Architecture and honest scope

This is a small interpretable generative completion model, not a diffusion model or proof of conscious experience. Three scale-specific dictionaries learn through winner-take-all normalized Hebbian-style updates. The full 8×8 patch decomposes exactly into a 2×2 base, 4×4 intermediate residue and 8×8 fine residue. Generating a hidden patch uses a learned coarse code, the most frequent matching conditional middle code, and a conditional fine residue.

Spatial templates are shared across all 12×12 patch positions and test candidate one- and two-part relations. One-patch geometric slack allows a limited form of deformation; this is not a learned continuous warp or a semantic object detector. Candidates win on predictive lift and support, not labels. Uploaded images remain in your browser and are not stored remotely.

The hard problems remain: real-world scale/viewpoint invariance, discovery of higher-order objects without combinatorial explosion, meaningful conditional high-frequency generation, and consistent results against interpolation and convolutional baselines. The included held-out real-photo benchmark may reveal negative transfer. Failed gates are still results.
